# Introduction
This project investigates inflation during the years of Joe Biden's presidency, comparing inflation in the United States to other advanced economies. Inflation is a critical economic issue that affects prices, purchasing power, and overall economic stability.

During President Biden’s term, inflation has been a central concern, with significant increases in prices across various sectors, including consumer goods, energy, and housing. This causal analysis seeks to examine whether inflation was worse during Biden's presidency than previous years compared to other economies.

##  :beginner: About
The data for this project can be found here at [International Monetary Fund](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/PCPIPCH@WEO/OEMDC/ADVEC/WEOWORLD)
